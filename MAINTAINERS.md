## How partner approval works

We use a **light-touch governance model**. No CLAs, no contributor agreements. The model:

1. **Open a Partner Application** (issue template). Tell us the area, your background, what you'd want to own.
2. **Trial period** — ship 2-3 PRs in the area. We'll review and merge as normal contributions.
3. **Maintainer invite** — if the trial PRs are quality, you get write access to the relevant area via `CODEOWNERS`.
4. **Decision authority** — maintainers can approve and merge PRs in their area without needing the founder's review (subject to standard review etiquette).

## Maintainer responsibilities

- Review PRs in your area within 7 days (a "no, here's why" within 7 days is fine; ghosting is not)
- Land at least 1 PR per quarter to stay active
- Respond to issues tagged for your area within 14 days
- Be a good citizen of the project per [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## Maintainer privileges

- Write access to the repo (scoped via `CODEOWNERS`)
- Listed on this page and in `README.md` Credits
- Decision authority in your area
- We hand you the keys, not just the password

## Conflict resolution

Disagreements are resolved by:

1. Public discussion on the PR/issue
2. If unresolved after 7 days: founder (Glincker) breaks tie
3. Code of Conduct violations: see [CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.md)

## Inactive maintainers

If a maintainer goes quiet for 6+ months without notice, we'll DM, then move them to "alumni" status with thanks. You can come back anytime.

## Why this isn't just "PRs welcome"

Most OSS projects say "PRs welcome" and then close PRs without merge (we've all seen that play out — it's literally why this repo exists). Real partnership means **handing over decision authority**, not just inviting code drops.

If you ship 3 quality PRs in an area, you should own that area. That's the deal.
